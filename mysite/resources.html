Helpful Cybersecurity Resources

The following websites provide useful information about cybersecurity and staying safe online.

  • CISA

    Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency

    CISA provides information about cybersecurity, online safety, and protecting critical systems.

    Why it is useful: It is a trusted government resource with current cybersecurity guidance and safety information.

  • National Institute of Standards and Technology

    NIST Cybersecurity

    NIST provides cybersecurity standards, guidelines, and information for organizations and individuals.

    Why it is useful: It helps people understand recommended cybersecurity practices and security standards.

  • Federal Trade Commission

    FTC Online Security

    The FTC provides advice about protecting personal information and avoiding online scams.

    Why it is useful: It provides practical advice that everyday internet users can use to protect themselves.

  • Cybersecurity and Privacy

    Khan Academy Computer Science

    Khan Academy offers educational material that can help students understand computers and the internet.

    Why it is useful: The lessons are designed for learning and can help beginners understand technical concepts.

  • Cybersecurity Awareness

    National Cybersecurity Alliance

    The National Cybersecurity Alliance provides information about online safety, privacy, passwords, and cybersecurity.

    Why it is useful: It provides simple cybersecurity tips that people can use in their everyday lives.
